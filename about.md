Automation Intel is a working notebook about build automation, developer
tooling, data pipelines and the practical side of applied intelligence —
the kind of notes that are worth writing down once and looking up later.

## What you'll find here

- Short, concrete write-ups of things that were annoying to figure out.
- Tooling comparisons with the trade-offs actually spelled out.
- Occasional longer pieces on how a system was put together.

## Colophon

This site is a [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) site hosted on
[GitHub Pages](https://pages.github.com/). There is no theme gem and no
JavaScript — just a handful of Liquid templates and one stylesheet, so it is
easy to read and easy to change.
